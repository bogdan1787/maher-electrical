Solar Grants, Explained Simply

The SEAI solar PV grant is designed to support the installation of new solar panels in Irish homes and make renewable energy more accessible. The scheme is popular, and its rules and rates are updated from time to time — so it pays to work from current information rather than last year's headlines.

Maher Electrical is SEAI registered and installs solar PV, battery storage and EV chargers across Co. Laois, Co. Offaly, Co. Kildare and the Midlands. We handle the paperwork on our side, guide you through the application and confirm the current grant position for your property before any work starts. The Basics

Here is what matters most before you consider applying. The fine detail is set by SEAI and can change, so treat this as a starting point — not a substitute for checking your own property.

  • Registered installers — grant-supported solar PV must be installed by an SEAI-registered contractor, which is exactly what we are.
  • Approval before work starts — the application needs to be approved before installation begins, so we never start early and put your grant at risk.
  • Conditions on the property and works — SEAI sets conditions covering the property, the system and how it is installed.
  • Battery storage — batteries may be supported where the scheme conditions are met; we check whether your proposed setup qualifies.
  • New installations — the grant is for new installations rather than systems that are already in place.
  • Current rates and conditions — SEAI sets the current rates, and they change over time. We confirm the position for your property at survey stage.

How the Application Works

Four straightforward stages — and we are with you at every one of them.

1. Free Survey

We visit your property, assess the roof, shading, existing electrics and your energy use, then recommend a system that suits your home.

2. Eligibility Check & Paperwork

We check your property against the current SEAI conditions and prepare the installer documentation needed for your application.

3. Approval Before Works

You submit the application with our details, and we wait for approval to be confirmed before ordering or installing anything.

4. Installation & Sign-Off

We install the system, test and certify it, and provide the documentation needed to complete the grant process.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Most grant problems come down to a handful of avoidable errors. Here are the ones we see most often.

  • Starting work before approval — work that begins before approval can put the grant at risk. We wait for the green light, and we advise you to do the same.
  • Using a non-registered installer — grant-supported works must be carried out by an SEAI-registered contractor with the correct documentation.
  • Assuming the grant covers everything — a grant is a contribution towards the works. We set out the full picture in your quote so there are no surprises.
  • Skipping BER and insulation advice — home energy upgrades work best together. It is worth checking whether BER or insulation measures could improve your overall savings before you invest in solar.
  • Not asking about battery storage — a battery lets you use more of your own solar power, especially in the evening. Ask us whether storage is supported under the current scheme and whether it suits your usage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the SEAI solar PV grant.

Who is eligible for the SEAI solar grant?

No two properties are the same, so we check rather than assume. SEAI sets the eligibility conditions, which typically cover the type and age of the property, ownership and any previous grant support. These conditions change over time, and we check your property against the current rules at the free survey — before you apply — so you know exactly where you stand.

Do I apply for the grant or does the installer?

Under the current scheme the homeowner usually submits the application, while the registered installer provides the technical details, documentation and certification. We walk you through each step and supply everything needed from our side, so nothing holds up your approval.

Can I get the grant if I add battery storage?

Battery storage may be supported where the scheme conditions are met, and SEAI sets those conditions. We will check whether your proposed system and property qualify under the current rules and advise on the best setup for how your home uses power.

Can I get the grant on a new build?

SEAI sets the rules around new builds, and these can change. Rather than guess, we check your property against the current conditions during the free survey and confirm what, if anything, is available before any work is planned.

How long does approval take?

Approval timelines are set by SEAI and vary with the volume of applications they are handling. We submit your documentation promptly, keep you updated, and never start work until approval is confirmed — that is what protects your grant.

What happens if my plans change after approval?

If the scope changes — for example, adding battery storage or adjusting the system size — the approval may need to be updated before the revised works go ahead. Tell us as early as possible and we will advise on the correct next step and handle the paperwork.
