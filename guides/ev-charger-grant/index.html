Charger Grants, Explained Simply

Home charger grants can reduce the cost of installing an EV charge point at your home, but the schemes come with conditions. Rates, approved products and eligibility rules are updated from time to time, so the detail matters more than the headline.

Maher Electrical is an experienced EV charger installer working across Co. Laois, Co. Offaly, Co. Kildare and the Midlands. We survey your property, confirm the current grant position and handle the paperwork on our side. For the wider picture, see our EV charger service.

The Basics

These are the points that decide most applications. The specifics are set by SEAI and can change, so we verify everything for your property before you apply.

  • Approved installers — grant-supported installations generally need to be carried out by an approved or registered installer, which we are.
  • Approved chargers — the charger itself generally needs to be on the scheme's approved list; we supply models that meet the current requirements.
  • Vehicle and property conditions — schemes set conditions around the vehicle, the property and where the charger is installed.
  • Smart charging — smart charging capability matters under the current schemes, and the chargers we fit are built for it.
  • One grant per property or vehicle — limits may apply depending on the scheme conditions in place, so we check the current position for you.
  • Rates set by SEAI — rates and conditions change over time. We confirm what applies to your home before you apply.

Why Professional Installation Matters

An EV charger is a high-current appliance, so it deserves more than a quick fit. A proper installation starts with a safe load assessment of your existing supply, correct cable sizing for the run and RCD protection suited to EV charging.

That work should be carried out by a registered, insured electrician and certified on completion — it protects you, your car and your warranty. If your fuse board or earthing needs attention first, we will tell you honestly and include it clearly in the quote. Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about home EV charger grants.

Who qualifies for the home EV charger grant?

SEAI sets the eligibility conditions, which generally cover the property, the vehicle and the charger being installed. Conditions change over time, so we check your details against the current scheme at the free home survey and confirm the position before you apply.

Which EV chargers qualify for the grant?

Chargers generally need to be on the approved list for the current scheme. We supply and fit well-known smart chargers that meet the scheme requirements, and we will confirm which models qualify for your installation at survey stage.

Does the grant cover the full cost of installation?

Generally, a grant is a contribution towards the cost of the works rather than a full payment. The exact position depends on the current scheme conditions and your property. We set everything out clearly in your quote and confirm the current grant position before work starts.

Can I install a charger without the grant?

Yes. You can have a charger installed without applying for grant support, and many customers choose that route when a scheme's conditions do not suit their situation. We will still carry out the survey, install safely and certify the work.

Can I get a grant for a second charger?

Support for a second charger depends on the scheme conditions in place at the time, including limits around the property and the vehicle. We check the current rules for you and explain your options before you decide.

How long does the process take?

It depends on approval timelines, charger availability and when the installation can be scheduled. We give you a realistic timeline at quote stage, keep you informed along the way and confirm dates before ordering anything.
