Industrial Electrical Services

Maher Electrical provides industrial electrical work for farms, workshops and industrial units across Laois, Offaly and Kildare. Based in Mountmellick, the service includes three-phase installations, machinery hook-ups, distribution boards and industrial maintenance.

Industrial projects depend on the machinery, supply and existing installation. Contact us to discuss the work and arrange a site assessment so the requirements can be established before a quote is prepared.
