What We Check

An inspection is a methodical look at the whole installation, not a quick glance at the fuse board. We test and record the condition of each part of the system, then explain what we found and what it means for you.

  • Consumer unit / fuse board condition, including protective devices.
  • Earthing and bonding of gas, water and other services.
  • Socket and lighting circuits throughout the property.
  • RCD trip tests to confirm protection operates correctly.
  • Insulation resistance and continuity testing on circuits.
  • External, garden and outbuilding circuits.
  • Remedial advice in plain English, with clear priorities.

If the report identifies an outdated fuse board or wiring that is nearing the end of its life, we can carry out the rewiring or upgrade and certify the finished work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about electrical inspections and certification.

Do I need an electrical certificate to sell my house?

There is no single document that every house sale legally requires, but buyers, solicitors and lenders often ask for evidence that the electrical installation is safe. An Installation Condition Report from a registered contractor provides that evidence. If issues are found, we can carry out the remedial work and certify it, and we can advise what is appropriate for your sale.

Do landlords need electrical inspections?

Landlords have safety obligations to keep the electrical installation in a rental property in a safe condition. The exact requirements depend on the property and the tenancy, and we are not legal advisers — but we carry out the inspections and reports landlords need and can advise what your property requires.

How often should a property be inspected?

It depends on the age and type of the property, how it is used and whether it is rented. An older installation or a rental property generally needs more regular attention than a recently rewired home. Rather than giving a one-size-fits-all answer, we will advise what is appropriate for your property and flag anything that should be checked sooner.

What does an Installation Condition Report include?

An Installation Condition Report records the condition of the installation, including the consumer unit, earthing and bonding, socket and lighting circuits, RCD protection and test results such as insulation resistance and continuity. Each observation is coded so you can see what is safe, what needs improvement and what should be dealt with urgently, along with practical advice in plain English.

How long does an inspection take?

A typical domestic inspection takes a few hours, depending on the size of the property and the number of circuits. Larger homes, farms and commercial premises take longer. We agree the scope beforehand and confirm how long we expect to be on site when you book.

Can you fix issues found during the inspection?

Yes. We can quote for and carry out any remedial work identified — from replacing a fuse board to rewiring circuits or upgrading earthing and bonding. Every job is different, so we assess the installation and give you a free quote before any work starts. If the issue is urgent, see our emergency electrician service.
