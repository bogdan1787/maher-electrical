Solar PV & Zappi EV Charger

New Build Property — Portlaoise, Co. Laois

10x 455W panels · 5kW inverter · 5kWh battery · Zappi 7kW charger

How the System Works

The solar PV system generates energy and prioritises the home usage or load first. If your usage is more than what is being generated, your battery supplies and makes up the balance. If there is excess PV power it charges the battery — and any excess after that goes directly to charge your EV via the Zappi when in eco mode.

This homeowner has only charged his EV via solar power since the install.

  • Energy independence and electricity savings from day one
  • Backup socket that can be used in the event of a grid failure or power outage, using your battery to supply the home
  • Zappi 7kW EV charger
  • All controlled, scheduled and monitored via the app
