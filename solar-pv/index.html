Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about solar PV and the SEAI grant.

Are you SEAI registered for solar PV?

Yes. Maher Electrical is an SEAI registered installer based in Mountmellick, Co. Laois. We are also ECSSA registered electrical contractors and fully insured, and every solar PV system we install is designed, commissioned and certified to current Irish standards.

Can I get the SEAI solar grant?

In most cases, yes — the SEAI solar PV grant is available to homeowners who use an SEAI registered installer, with separate support for battery storage. Grant rates and eligibility rules change over time, so we confirm the current position for your property at survey and quote stage and handle the paperwork with you. Our solar grant guide explains what is typically involved.

How many solar panels does my house need?

There is no one-size-fits-all answer — it depends on your annual electricity use, the size, shape and orientation of your roof, and whether you plan to add a battery or charge an EV. During your free survey we review your usage and roof, then design a system that makes sense for your home rather than simply filling the roof.

Do you need a battery with my solar panels?

No — solar PV works perfectly well without a battery, and any surplus is exported to the grid. A battery lets you store daytime generation for the evening, which suits households that are out during the day or want more independence from the grid. We will explain the trade-offs during your survey so you can decide what is right for your home or farm.

Can I charge my EV with solar power?

Yes. With a Zappi charger fitted alongside your solar PV, your car can charge on surplus solar generation instead of imported electricity — the Portlaoise project in our portfolio does exactly that. We install Zappi chargers and integrate them with your solar and battery system so you get the most from your own power.

How long does a solar installation take?

Most residential solar PV installations are completed in one to two days, depending on the size of the system, the roof and whether battery storage is included. We confirm the schedule at quote stage, keep you informed on the day, and commission the system and set up monitoring before we leave. Grant paperwork is submitted promptly afterwards.
