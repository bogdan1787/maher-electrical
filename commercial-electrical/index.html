Commercial Electrical Work

Maher Electrical carries out electrical installations for shops, offices, pubs, workshops and other commercial premises. Based in Mountmellick, Co. Laois, we serve businesses across Laois, Offaly and Kildare.

Work can include new installations and fit-outs, lighting and power, distribution boards, fault finding, maintenance, testing and certification. We agree the job scope before work begins and can discuss scheduling around your business operations.
