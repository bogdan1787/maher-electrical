Your Local Electrician in Birr

Birr is one of the midlands’ best-known heritage towns, and its older buildings shape a lot of the work we do there. Maher Electrical looks after period and older properties across the town — full and partial rewires, fuse board upgrades, and earthing and bonding brought up to current standards — as well as the shops, pubs and offices that keep the town centre busy.

Beyond the town, we look after Crinkill and the farming parishes of south Offaly. That means three-phase connections, shed and yard lighting and machinery hook-ups on working farms, plus solar PV and battery storage for farm buildings and rural businesses that want to cut their energy overheads. EV charger installations are growing steadily around Birr too, for drivers in town and visitors to the area. Every job is ECSSA certified and we are SEAI approved for grant-supported renewable work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from customers in Birr and south Offaly.

Do you cover Birr and the surrounding area?

Yes. We cover Birr town and the surrounding parishes, including Crinkill, Banagher, Cloghan, Kilcormac and Shinrone, as well as the wider county through our Co. Offaly service.

Can you rewire an older or period property in Birr?

Yes. Older housing stock makes up a large part of our work in Birr. We carry out full and partial rewires, replace old fuse boxes with modern consumer units, and sort out earthing and bonding, with full certification on completion. See our rewiring service for more detail.

Do you carry out farm electrical work around Birr?

Yes. We look after farms across south Offaly — three-phase connections, shed and yard lighting, machinery hook-ups and safety switches, as well as solar PV on farm buildings.

Do you install solar PV and EV chargers in Birr?

Yes. We are SEAI registered and install solar PV, battery storage and EV chargers for Birr homes, farms and businesses. We carry out a free survey and handle the SEAI grant paperwork, including confirming the current grant position before work starts.

Can I get an electrical inspection or certificate for a property in Birr?

Yes. We issue Installation Condition Reports and electrical certification for house sales, landlords and older properties in Birr and the surrounding area. Have a look at our inspection and certification service, or call us to arrange a visit.
