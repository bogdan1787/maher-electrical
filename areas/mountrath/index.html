Your Local Electrician in Mountrath

Mountrath is a busy market town in south Laois on the N7 corridor between Portlaoise and Borris-in-Ossory, sitting at the gateway to the Slieve Bloom mountains. It is a town with a strong farming hinterland, where the streets and the townlands around them depend on the same practical trades. Maher Electrical works in Mountrath regularly from our base in neighbouring Mountmellick, so you get a contractor who knows the area and the properties in it.

That mix shows in the work we do here: older cottages and farmhouses that are due a rewire or a fuse board upgrade, farmyards that need power to sheds and outbuildings, and newer homes and one-off builds looking at solar PV and EV charging. Every job is certified, and as an ECSSA registered, SEAI approved contractor, the paperwork is done properly from the first call to completion.
