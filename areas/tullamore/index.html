Your Local Electrician in Tullamore

As the county town of Offaly, Tullamore is used to doing business. The town centre mixes period town houses and shops with modern estates spreading towards the bypass, while the retail parks, offices and industrial units around Clonminch and Sragh keep the place working through the week. With direct rail links and the Grand Canal on its doorstep, it is as much a commuter base now as a market town.

Maher Electrical serves that mix of old and new — from rewires and fuse board upgrades in older town-centre properties to commercial fit-outs and solar PV and EV charger installations in the newer estates and business parks. We are ECSSA registered and SEAI approved, based half an hour away in Mountmellick, and every job is certified before we leave.
