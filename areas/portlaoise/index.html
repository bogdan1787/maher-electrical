Portlaoise is the county town of Laois and the largest town in the county — a busy commuter base on the M7 with a real mix of buildings. Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from customers in Portlaoise.

Do you cover Portlaoise town and the surrounding estates?

Yes. Portlaoise is one of our main service towns. We cover the town centre and estates such as Kilminchy, Rathevan and Esker Hills, as well as Stradbally, Timahoe, Ballyroan, Ballybrittas, Killenard and The Heath. See our Co. Laois service for the rest of the county.

How quickly can you attend an emergency call-out in Portlaoise?

We are based in Mountmellick, a short run from Portlaoise, and provide call-out service seven days a week for tripping switches, power loss and electrical faults. Call or WhatsApp 085 200 8988 and we will give you an honest arrival time.

Do you install solar PV and EV chargers for Portlaoise homes?

Yes. We are SEAI registered and install solar PV, battery storage and home EV chargers, including Zappi units that use surplus solar power. One of our recent Portlaoise projects combined solar panels, battery storage and a Zappi charger. See our solar PV and EV charger services for details.

Can you upgrade the fuse board in an older Portlaoise house?

Yes. Many older town-centre and estate houses still have dated boards. We replace them with modern consumer units including RCD/RCBO protection and surge protection, issue certification, and can assess the electrics for house sales or rental properties. See our fuse board upgrade service.

Do you wire new builds and renovations around Portlaoise?

Yes. We carry out first and second fix wiring for new builds in Portlaoise's growing estates, along with extensions and renovations. All work is certified and scheduled to suit your builder's programme. See our rewiring service for more.
