Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cover all of Co. Laois?

Yes. Maher Electrical is based in Mountmellick and works throughout Co. Laois — including Portlaoise, Abbeyleix, Portarlington, Stradbally, Rathdowney, Mountrath and Durrow — as well as the surrounding counties of Offaly, Kildare, Carlow and Kilkenny.

Do you install solar PV in Co. Laois and can I get the SEAI grant?

Yes. We are SEAI registered and install solar PV systems for homes, farms and businesses across Laois. SEAI grant amounts change over time — get in touch and we will confirm the current grant rate for your property and handle the paperwork as part of your free survey and quote.

Do you provide emergency electrician call-outs in Laois?

Yes. We provide fast fault finding and call-out service seven days a week for tripping switches, power outages and electrical faults in Laois homes and businesses. Call or WhatsApp 085 200 8988.

Yes. Maher Electrical is registered with ECSSA (Electrical Contractors Safety & Standards Association) and fully insured. All works are certified, and Installation Condition Reports and completion certificates are issued for every job.
