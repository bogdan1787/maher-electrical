Your Local Electrician in Edenderry

Edenderry sits at the northern tip of Offaly, a short spin from the Kildare and Meath borders. Once a market town on the Grand Canal, it has grown into a busy commuter base, with new housing estates filling out alongside older streets, cottages and farmhouses. The canal still threads along the edge of town, and the farming hinterland around Rhode, Croghan and Clonbullogue is never more than a few minutes away.

That spread of work shapes what we do here. New homeowners ask about EV chargers and solar PV; older properties need rewires, fuse board upgrades and safe, certified fixes; and farms and businesses want dependable power. Maher Electrical is ECSSA registered and SEAI approved, covering Edenderry and north Offaly from our Mountmellick base, with every job certified on completion.
