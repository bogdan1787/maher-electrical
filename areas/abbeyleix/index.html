Your Local Electrician in Abbeyleix

Abbeyleix is one of Ireland's heritage towns, laid out in the 18th century with a streetscape and stone buildings that have been carefully preserved. That character is a big part of why people love living here — and it shapes the electrical work the town needs. Maher Electrical carries out work in Abbeyleix and the surrounding countryside from our Mountmellick base, treating older buildings with the care they deserve.

Period houses come with original or long-outdated wiring, so we spend time assessing what is safe to keep and what needs replacing. Typical jobs include careful rewiring, fuse board upgrades and certification for house sales, alongside solar PV and EV charger installations for the town and the farmland around it. All work is certified by an ECSSA registered, SEAI approved contractor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from customers in Abbeyleix.

Do you cover Abbeyleix and the surrounding townlands?

Yes. We work in Abbeyleix town and the surrounding area, including Ballinakill, Ballyroan, Shanahoe, Raheen and Spink, as well as the wider Co. Laois area from our Mountmellick base. Our Co. Laois service covers the full county.

Do you have experience rewiring period homes in Abbeyleix?

Yes. Abbeyleix has one of Ireland's best-preserved heritage townscapes, and much of its housing stock predates modern wiring. We rewire period homes carefully, upgrading fuse boards, earthing and circuits while respecting the fabric of the building, and certify all work on completion. See our rewiring service for more.

Is an EV charger suitable for an older Abbeyleix house?

In most cases, yes. We check the capacity of your existing supply and fuse board first, upgrade what is needed, and then install and commission a charger sized to your home. Zappi chargers can also use surplus solar power if you have panels — see our EV charger service.

Can you provide electrical certificates for a house sale in Abbeyleix?

Yes. We carry out Installation Condition Reports and issue certification for house sales, landlords and pre-renovation surveys. If remedial work is needed, we explain what is required and can quote for it — call or WhatsApp 085 200 8988 to arrange a visit.

Do you install solar PV in the Abbeyleix area?

Yes. We are SEAI registered and install solar PV and battery storage for homes and farms around Abbeyleix. Every job starts with a free survey, and we confirm the current SEAI grant position before any work begins.
