Your Local Electrician in Athy

Athy sits where the River Barrow meets the Grand Canal, and it is one of south Kildare’s busiest commuter towns. That mix shows in the work we do here — wiring and certifying new-build homes in the growing estates, upgrading fuse boards and rewiring older town houses, and fitting home EV chargers for people who travel to Dublin, Kildare or Carlow for work. The rail station and the M9 make the daily commute straightforward, and a home charger makes it work out cheaper.

Out beyond the town we look after farms and rural homes towards Ballylinan, Maganey and the Barrow valley, where shed wiring, three-phase work and solar PV are regular requests. Maher Electrical is ECSSA registered and SEAI approved, so whether it is a new build, an older property or a farm, the work is completed and certified properly from first fix to final sign-off.
