Your Local Electrician in Carlow

Carlow Town is the county town and a genuine commercial hub for the south-east midlands — retail parks, offices, professional services and industrial units on both sides of the River Barrow. A good share of our Carlow work is commercial: fit-outs, additional circuits, distribution boards and maintenance for businesses that need a contractor who works cleanly, keeps to schedule and certifies everything.

Carlow is growing as well, with new residential developments alongside older town properties that predate modern wiring standards. For older homes we carry out rewires, fuse board upgrades and inspections; for newer builds and commuters we install solar PV, battery storage and EV chargers. We cover Tinryland, Bennekerry and Ballon to the east, and Tullow, Bagenalstown and the Barrow valley to the south. Every job is ECSSA certified and we are SEAI approved for grant-supported renewable work.
